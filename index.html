Undergraduate Researcher | Computer Science @ Sun Yat-sen University

School of Computer Science and Engineering

Sun Yat-sen University

Guangzhou, China

I am Yijia Fan, an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Sun Yat-sen University, supervised by Prof. Keze Wang. I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.

I am a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia – Shanghai AI/ML Group, working on video generation and discretized VLM projects. I have previously interned at the Human Cyber-Physical Intelligence Lab at Sun Yat-sen University and participated in the X-Era & Sun Yat-sen University Pop Mart Enterprise Project on VLM-based defect detection with RLHF.

My research interests include visual language models, multi-modal intelligence, reinforcement learning, and agentic systems. I am particularly interested in aligning visual perception with complex reasoning and exploring unified generation toward a world model that integrates perception, reasoning, and creation. I’m open to any internship opportunities.

Contact: fanyj28@mail2.sysu.edu.cn
