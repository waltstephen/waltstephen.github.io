I am Yijia Fan, a fourth-year undergraduate in Computer Science and Technology at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn), supervised by [Prof. Keze Wang](https://kezewang.com/). I will join [MMLab](https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/) as a Ph.D. student, advised by [Prof. Xiangyu Yue](https://xyue.io/).

I am a research intern at [Microsoft Research Asia – Shanghai AI/ML Group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/), working on video generation, unified multimodal models, agent skills, and recursive self-improvement (self-evolving agents). From June to September 2026, I was a research intern with the SenseNova team at [SenseTime](https://www.sensetime.com/), where I worked on native reflection in unified models with reinforcement learning ([UMM-Reflection](https://waltstephen.github.io/UMM-Reflection/)). I have previously interned at the [Human Cyber-Physical Intelligence Lab](https://www.sysu-hcp.net/) at Sun Yat-sen University and participated in the X-Era & Sun Yat-sen University [Pop Mart](https://www.popmart.com/) Enterprise Project on VLM-based defect detection with RLHF.

My research interests include unified multimodal models, visual language models, reinforcement learning, and self-evolving agentic systems. I am particularly interested in aligning visual perception with complex reasoning and exploring unified generation toward a world model that integrates perception, reasoning, and creation.
