I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn), supervised by [Prof. Keze Wang](https://sites.google.com/view/kezewang). I am seeking **PhD opportunities for Fall 2027**.

My research is anchored in the continuous evolution of **Visual Language Models (VLMs)**, synergizing them with the advanced agentic capabilities of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** to construct robust multi-modal intelligence. I focus on aligning visual perception with complex reasoning via **reinforcement learning**. Building on my experience with LLaVA architectures and post-training techniques like RFT, I concurrently explore pathways for unified generation. Ultimately, my long-term objective is to forge a unified world model that seamlessly integrates perception, agentic reasoning, and generative creation.

Currently, I am a research intern at [Microsoft Research Asia - Shanghai ML Group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/), working on video generation and next-generation discretized VLM projects. I have also interned at the [Human Cyber Physical Intelligence Integration Lab](http://www.csbio.info/) at Sun Yat-sen University and participated in the PopMart Enterprise Project with X-Era.
